A launcher for Android

Your home screen, your shape.

Morph is a home screen that takes the shape you give it. Start from one of five looks, then change anything: where your apps live, how your icons are drawn, what your widgets show and what sits behind them.

Five looks to start from

Classic, Library, Minimal, Index or Shelves. Each pairs a home screen with a way to reach your apps, and every part of it can be changed later in Settings.

Keep your apps as an A to Z list, a grid, pages, category pages or category cards.

Swipe to any screen

The Screen manager puts a screen on every edge of your home screen. Swipe to your apps, your categories or your pages from whichever side you want, with the animation you want.

Design your own wallpapers

The wallpaper studio makes generative wallpapers: gradients, waves, facets, planets, auroras and more, in ready-made palettes or your own colors, with filters on top.

Or use any picture you like.

Make icons your own

The icon studio is a layer editor for app icons. Change the shape, add layers and effects, and style every app at once, or give one app a look of its own.

Watercolor, paper, glass and more than twenty other effects. Clock and calendar icons stay live.

Widgets you can restyle

Clock, weather, calendar, music, notifications, battery, photos and more. Every widget can be restyled down to its blocks, colors and fonts, and you can build one from scratch.

Group icons or widgets into containers, and place widgets on the grid or position them freely.

Liquid glass, Glacier, blur or solid

Choose how panels sit over your wallpaper, so folders, containers and the app screen match the picture behind them.

Made for every screen

Phones, tablets and foldables, in portrait and landscape. A foldable keeps a separate layout for its inner and cover screens, or shows the cover screen's home screen across the inner one.

And the rest of a home screen

Gestures

Swipes, double tap and two-finger gestures, each opening an app, a shortcut or a system action.

Notification dots

A dot or a count on any app with something waiting, in a shape and color of your choosing.

Folders and containers

Folders on any screen, and containers that hold a set of icons or a stack of widgets.

Share a design

Send an icon look, a wallpaper or a widget as a link. The design travels inside the link, with no server.

Hide the bars

Hide the status and navigation bars on the home screen for a clean picture.

31 languages

Morph's own language can be set apart from your phone's, on every Android version it runs on.

Collects nothing about you

No account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Your layout, designs and settings stay on your device.

Weather forecasts come straight from MET Norway, and Morph asks for a permission only when you turn on the feature that needs it.

Free, with optional extras

The launcher and all its layouts are free, with free widgets and wallpapers to start with. More widgets, studio designs, effects and gestures come with Morph Premium or one-time packs.
