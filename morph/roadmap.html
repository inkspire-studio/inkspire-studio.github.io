Search

Search your contacts, files and calendar, and by voice

Search will also find your contacts, files and calendar events, and you'll be able to speak your search instead of typing it. Contacts, files, the calendar and the microphone are used only once you allow them, and Morph never asks just because you opened search.

Accessibility

Works with TalkBack

Every app, folder and widget on Morph's screens reachable and openable with TalkBack and other accessibility services, and your home screen arranged without dragging.

Wallpapers and widgets

Backgrounds that change on their own

Pick a few designs for your wallpaper or a widget's background, and Morph moves to the next one every few minutes, or at the times of day you set: one look for the morning, another for the evening.

Already in Morph

The main features in Morph today, the newest first.

  • Sorting and suggestionsKeep your apps A–Z, by how often you open them, or in your own order, with a row of the apps you usually open at this time of day. What Morph counts stays on your phone, and you can clear it.
  • SearchFind apps, app shortcuts, Morph's settings and your phone's settings from any app layout, a swipe of its own or a search bar on your home screen, and send what you typed to Google, the Play Store, YouTube, Maps or a search engine you add.
  • SharingSend an icon look, a wallpaper or a widget by link; the design travels inside it.
  • Immersive modeHide the status bar and the navigation bar, and let your icons and widgets use the space.
  • FoldablesSeparate layouts for the inner and cover screens, or the cover screen mirrored across the inner one.
  • TabletsLayouts made for a tablet's screen, that keep their arrangement when you turn it.
  • 30+ languagesMorph in 31 languages, chosen in its own settings on any Android version.
  • Widget studioRestyle any widget down to its blocks, add blocks from any design or your saved widgets, build one from scratch, use your own fonts, and keep your favorites in My widgets.
  • Portrait and landscapeA layout of its own for each orientation, or one that turns with the phone.
  • Wallpaper studioGenerative wallpapers in ready-made palettes or your own colors, with filters on top.
  • GesturesSwipe with one or two fingers, or double-tap, on the home screen or on any icon, to open an app or a shortcut, pull down notifications or quick settings, lock the screen or control music.
  • Icon studioRestyle any app's icon in layers, with shapes, effects and materials from watercolor to frozen glass, for one app or all of them.
  • Five ways to lay out your appsA list, a grid, pages, pages by category or category cards, each arranged and sized the way you like.
  • Two home screensPages with a dock, or a list beside a widget area, both arranged freely.
