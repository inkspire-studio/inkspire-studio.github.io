Search

Search everything

The search on the app screen will find more than apps: Morph's own settings, system settings, app shortcuts, contacts, files and calendar events. It can also hand what you typed to Google, the Play Store, YouTube or Maps, and you'll be able to speak your search instead of typing it. Contacts, files, the calendar and the microphone are used only once you allow them, and Morph never asks just because you opened search.

Accessibility

Works with TalkBack

Every app, folder and widget on Morph's screens reachable and openable with TalkBack and other accessibility services, and your home screen arranged without dragging.

Wallpapers and widgets

Backgrounds that change on their own

Pick a few designs for your wallpaper or a widget's background, and Morph moves to the next one every few minutes, or at the times of day you set: one look for the morning, another for the evening.
